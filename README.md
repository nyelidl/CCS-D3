## About

**CCS-D³ Bridge 2027 × QiLS** is a multidisciplinary scientific forum connecting researchers and students across computational science, data science, molecular science, and life science.

The forum aims to create interactions across methods, disciplines, and research questions, bringing together perspectives from:

- Artificial Intelligence / Machine Learning / LLMs
- Scientific Data and Knowledge Integration
- Molecular Dynamics and Molecular Simulation
- Molecular Docking
- Quantum Chemistry
- QM/MM
- Fragment Molecular Orbital (FMO)
- Structural Biology
- Molecular Biology
- Predictive Modeling
- Experiment–Computation Integration

> **Predict. Discuss. Discover What Comes Next.**

## 7+11 Research Pitch

**7 — PITCH**  
7 minutes to present the research question, current evidence, and scientific challenge.

**11 — DISCUSS**  
11 minutes of multidisciplinary scientific discussion.

The goal is to move beyond presentation and identify the **next question, calculation, experiment, or collaboration**.
