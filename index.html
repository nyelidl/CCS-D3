12 — PARTICIPANT STREAM

JST SAKURA SCIENCE × QiLS

CCS-D³ Bridge 2027 brings together two major participant communities: researchers from the Center for Quantum and Information Life Sciences (QiLS), University of Tsukuba, and young researchers participating in the JST Sakura Science Program “Japan–Thailand Advanced Molecular Simulation Training for Predictive Research Design.”

Approximately twelve young researchers from Thailand will join QiLS researchers, University of Tsukuba students, research mentors, and external participants in a shared scientific forum focused on discussion, collaboration, and the development of new research questions, calculations, and experiments.
